## Project Summary

The **Road Accident Data Analysis (India)** project is a Python-based data analysis project that studies recorded road accident data from 2021 to 2024. The project follows a structured eight-stage workflow covering data loading, acquisition, extraction, validation, cleaning, aggregation, analysis, visualization, and results interpretation.

The analysis examines accident patterns across different years and categories, including accident severity, major causes, vehicle types, speed-limit conditions, alcohol involvement, pedestrian involvement, and daylight conditions.

The project uses Python libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to process, analyze, and visualize the accident data. The results are presented through statistical summaries and visualizations to make important accident patterns easier to understand.

The project identified several notable patterns in the supplied dataset, including the highest recorded accident count in 2024, the prominence of fatal accidents, overspeeding as a leading recorded cause, and the significant involvement of two-wheelers.

Overall, the project demonstrates how a structured data-analysis workflow can be used to transform raw road accident records into meaningful analysis, visualizations, and insights.
