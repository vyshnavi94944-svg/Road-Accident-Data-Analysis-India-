# Stage 8 – Results and Interpretation

## Purpose

Interpret the results obtained from the road accident analysis and visualizations to identify important accident patterns, trends and observations.

## Required checks

- Review the results obtained from the data analysis stage.
- Interpret accident counts by state.
- Interpret fatalities by state.
- Interpret severe and minor injury patterns.
- Interpret total injury patterns.
- Compare accidents and fatalities.
- Compare accidents and total injuries.
- Examine important distributions shown in the visualizations.
- Identify major patterns and observations from the analysis.
- Summarize the key findings obtained from the project.
- Relate the findings to road accident analysis within the scope of the dataset.
- Document the limitations of the analysis.

**Important:** The interpretation is based on the actual results and visualizations generated from the project dataset. Conclusions are limited to the patterns observed in the available data.

## Output

The stage produces the final results, key observations and interpretation of the road accident analysis.

The findings can be used to summarize the project outcomes and support the conclusion of the Road Accident Data Analysis project.
